Core Behavioral Factors (Per Customer-Item Interaction)
These are extracted directly from CV tracking events. Each interaction is logged with:

1. Dwell Time (Duration in Zone)

Time spent looking at a specific item or zone (in seconds).

Baseline score: 0-10 seconds = 1pt, 10-30s = 3pts, 30-60s = 5pts, 60s+ = 10pts.

Higher dwell = stronger interest signal.
​

2. Pickup & Return Behavior

Item picked up (inspected closely): +5pts.

Item returned to shelf (reconsidered): -2pts.

Item left in hand (walk away from zone): -3pts.

Multiple pickups of same item: +7pts (high intent).

3. Zone Visit Frequency

How many unique customers visit a zone per hour.

Score factor: Zone_visits_per_hour / max_visits → normalized 0-10 scale.

Decathlon gym zone: 20 visits/hr = high traffic score.

4. Purchase Completion

Customer enters zone → picks up item → moves to checkout: +10pts (conversion).

Customer enters zone → browses → leaves without purchase: 0pts (interest only).

Conversion rate per zone: (purchases / visits) × 10.

6. Peak Hour Engagement

7. Customer Repeat Visits (Loyalty Proxy)

Same customer visits same zone/item multiple times.

Repeat score: 1st visit = 1x, 2nd = 1.2x, 3rd+ = 1.5x multiplier on engagement.

Indicates product loyalty or comparison-shopping behavior.

8. Visit Duration Trend (Engagement Momentum)

Track if dwell time increases/decreases over successive visits.

Increasing dwell = rising interest: +2pts per visit increase.

Decreasing dwell = fading interest: -1pt per visit decrease.

9. Queue/Congestion Effect

If zone is crowded (>5 customers simultaneously), dwell time artificially inflates.

Adjustment: Dwell time in crowded zones discounted by 20-30%.

Or flag as "high demand despite congestion" = strong signal.

Seasonal & Event-Based Factors
Account for festivals, holidays, and sports seasons when certain gear spikes.


Engagement & Conversion Factors
14. Engagement Score (Per Customer-Zone)

Composite of dwell, pickups, repeat visits, event proximity.

Formula:

text
Engagement_Score = (Dwell_pts × 0.3) + (Pickup_pts × 0.3) + 
                   (Repeat_Multiplier × 0.2) + (Event_Boost × 0.2)
Range: 0-100.

15. Conversion Probability (Predictive)

Use historical data: if dwell > 45s + pickup + zone visit before = 70% conversion likelihood.

Scoring rule:

High dwell + pickup + return visit pattern = +8pts toward purchase score.

Single visit, no pickup = +1pt (low conversion likelihood).

Zone-Level Aggregated Factors
17. Zone Popularity Score

Sum of all customer engagement scores in zone over a time window.

Formula: Zone_Score = Σ(Customer_Engagement) / hour → normalized 0-100.

Decathlon gym zone: 450 engagement points/hour = 90/100 popularity.

18. Zone Conversion Efficiency

(Total purchases in zone / total visits to zone) × 100.

High traffic + low conversion = redesign issue.

Low traffic + high conversion = niche/premium segment (e.g., pro cycling gear).

19. Cross-Zone Migration (Customer Flow)

Track if customer visits Zone A (running) then Zone B (gym).

Affinity score: If 60% of gym customers also visit running = cross-zone boost +3pts.

Indicates complementary categories; placement optimization.



23. Dwell-to-Purchase Conversion Funnel

Track: View → Dwell → Pickup → Purchase.

Dropout rate at each stage.

Predictive score: If 30% drop between "pickup" and "purchase," flag zone for checkout friction.

24. Customer Segment Engagement Variance

Segment customers by visit frequency (casual, repeat, frequent).

High engagement from frequent visitors = stable demand.

High engagement from casual visitors = viral/trending product.

Score adjustment: Frequent visitor engagement weighted 0.6x, casual visitor 1.2x (trending signal).

25. Anomaly/Outlier Detection

Sudden spike in dwell time or pickups (unusual for item/zone).

Flag rule: Engagement > (mean + 2 std dev) = anomaly.

Could indicate viral moment, influencer visit, or setup issue.

Apply dampening to avoid false inventory spikes.

Item-Level Scoring Summary
Final Item Score =

text
(Base_Engagement × 0.25) + 
(Conversion_Probability × 0.25) + 
(Trend_Velocity × 0.15) + 
(Seasonal_Multiplier × 0.15) + 
(Affinity_Strength × 0.10) + 
(Event_Boost × 0.10)
Range: 0-100 points.

Interpretation:

80-100: "Buy More Now" – trending, high demand.

50-79: "Stable Performer" – consistent interest.

20-49: "Declining Interest" – post-event decay or off-season.

0-19: "Low Priority" – overstock, niche appeal, or wrong placement.

Zone-Level Scoring Summary
Final Zone Score =

text
(Zone_Popularity × 0.30) + 
(Conversion_Efficiency × 0.25) + 
(Cross_Zone_Affinity × 0.20) + 
(Inventory_Health × 0.15) + 
(Trend_Velocity × 0.10)
Range: 0-100 points.

Interpretation:

80-100: "Premium Zone" – high traffic, high conversion, optimize layout.

50-79: "Standard Zone" – healthy, monitor for seasonal shifts.

20-49: "Underperforming" – review layout, product mix, staffing.

0-19: "Critical Zone" – poor inventory fit or placement error.
